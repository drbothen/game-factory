# Domain Invariants

> **Sharded L2 section (DF-021).** Navigate via `L2-INDEX.md`.
> Invariants are domain rules that must always hold — never violated by design.
> Each is grounded in the product brief or charter, not invented.

---

## DI-001 — Factory Core Never Names a Specific Engine

The factory core (Layers 1-2) NEVER imports an engine SDK, references an engine by name,
or assumes any engine capability. All engine knowledge is quarantined in Layer-4 Adapters.

DI-001 is a business invariant because the entire engine-agnostic thesis collapses if the
core develops any engine-specific coupling. Grounded in Brief §Success Criteria ("ZERO core
changes"), architecture.md §The split, and ADR-0002.

---

## DI-002 — Every Engine Adapter Must Pass Conformance Before Acceptance

No engine adapter may be used in factory production until it passes the conformance suite
for all of its declared capabilities. An adapter that claims a capability but fails its
conformance test is not accepted at any fidelity level.

DI-002 is a business invariant because it is the anti-drift mechanism — without it the
four-seam adapter pattern decays into engine-specific special cases. Grounded in ADR-0002
("conformance suite is the load-bearing anti-drift artifact") and Brief §Success Criteria.

---

## DI-003 — Every Generated Asset Has a Complete Provenance Sidecar

Every asset generated by the factory must have a provenance sidecar with all required
fields populated at generation time, including `disclosure_class`. An asset without a
complete sidecar may not be ingested into the asset store.

DI-003 is a business invariant because EU AI Act Art. 50 (C2PA marks, 2026-08-02),
Steam AI disclosure (Jan 2026), and copyright risk management all depend on sidecar
completeness. Grounded in Brief §Success Criteria ("100%; 0 missing `disclosure_class`"),
§Constraints, and RECONCILIATION §9.

---

## DI-004 — Determinism Tier Is Declared, Never Assumed

The factory never assumes a determinism tier for any adapter. Tier is declared in the
adapter manifest, conformance-verified, and used to select the appropriate regression
comparison method. An undeclared tier defaults to `tolerance-only`.

DI-004 is a business invariant because research confirmed determinism is opt-in on every
engine; assuming T1 for a T2/T3 adapter produces silent false-positive regression results.
Grounded in ADR-0003 and RECONCILIATION §C.1.

---

## DI-005 — Monetization Optimization Is Always Constrained

When monetization is present, the factory must have a `monetization-ethics-contract`
that is adversarially reviewed. The factory may never autonomously optimize for unconstrained
LTV maximization. Any agent or hook that produces unconstrained LTV optimization without
a declared ethics contract is a factory defect.

DI-005 is a business invariant because unconstrained LTV optimization is explicitly named
as a factory defect in the product brief ("unconstrained LTV maximize is a factory defect")
and the monetization-ethics dimension is one of 11 convergence gates. Grounded in Brief
§Constraints and RECONCILIATION §10, §12 R-010.

---

## DI-006 — Human-Gated Tasks Are Surfaced, Not Silently Dropped

When a production step reaches a `human-gated` fidelity boundary (console cert, store
publish, SAG-AFTRA consent, legal opinion, XR comfort-cert, paid-UGC vetting), the
factory must surface a checklisted human task. Suppressing or silently skipping this
task is a hook-detectable defect.

DI-006 is a business invariant because it preserves the honesty principle of the factory:
it does not pretend to automate what it cannot. Grounded in Brief §HUMAN-GATED external
steps, Brief §Constraints, and RECONCILIATION §5A ("The unifying human-gated principle").

---

## DI-007 — Playtest Satisfaction Is Always a Human Gate

The playtest-satisfaction convergence dimension is always a mandatory human gate. No
automated metric, scalar score, or agent output may substitute for the structured playtest
protocol human sign-off. Any automated "fun score" emitted by an agent or hook is a defect.

DI-007 is a business invariant because "auto-scoring fun" is explicitly listed as "OUT
(never)" in the product brief and reinforced by RECONCILIATION §10 Risk R-010 and §12.

---

## DI-008 — Factory Core Spec Layer Is Engine-Portable by Construction

Game specs (design, systems, economy, narrative) produced by the factory must be expressed
in engine-neutral terms. An engine-specific implementation construct (e.g., a Unity
MonoBehaviour reference, a Bevy ECS component type) may not appear in a spec-layer artifact.

DI-008 is a business invariant because "the spec layer is engine-portable by construction"
is an explicit architectural consequence in architecture.md ("A design can be re-targeted
to another engine even though its code/assets cannot port").

---

## DI-009 — Suno/Udio and Unlicensed AI Music Providers Are Blocked

The factory may not route asset generation to Suno, Udio, or other AI music generators
identified as litigation-exposed or ToS-excluded in the product brief. Factory defaults
to licensed providers (Stable Audio, AIVA, Soundraw).

DI-009 is a business invariant because these tools are identified as ToS-excluded in
the product brief ("Suno/Udio litigation") and represent R-003 (legal/audio risk).
Grounded in Brief §Constraints and RECONCILIATION §12 R-003.

---

## DI-010 — Kernel Anti-Cheat Is Never Autonomously Authored

The factory may never autonomously author kernel-mode anti-cheat drivers or ring-0 code.
Anti-cheat integration is wrap-only (EAC/EOS default; BattlEye commercial; Riot Vanguard
not licensable).

DI-010 is a business invariant because post-CrowdStrike, kernel AC is identified as beyond
factory scope and a high security risk (R-017). Riot Vanguard is explicitly non-licensable.
Grounded in Brief §Out of Scope and RECONCILIATION §12 R-017.

---

## DI-011 — NFT and Web3 Mechanics Are Off by Default

The factory does not enable NFT, blockchain token, or web3 mechanics by default. Any
activation requires an explicit `business-model-spec` declaration. NFT triggers PEGI 18
rating minimum (RECONCILIATION R-013).

DI-011 is a business invariant because NFT → PEGI 18 is a confirmed regulatory trigger
(Jun 2026) and web3/crypto is explicitly "off-by-default (risk + regulatory)" in
RECONCILIATION §6.3. Grounded in Brief §Constraints.

---

## DI-012 — Every ContractArtifact Has a Declared Validation Method

Every simulation BC, design-intent contract, replay-regression contract, and ethics
contract must declare its validation method at creation time. A contract without a
declared validation method is structurally invalid.

DI-012 is a business invariant because machine-checkability is the core value of the
contract-driven quality model. A contract that cannot be validated provides no quality
signal. Grounded in RECONCILIATION §6.1 (validation method column) and Brief §In Scope.
