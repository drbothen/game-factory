## Section 9: CAP-009 — Cert Pre-Flight and Distribution-Readiness (P1)

### 9.1 Overview

CAP-009 provides the factory's complete automatable prefix for getting a game build from
"built" to "delivered to storefronts / distribution targets," including all machine-checkable
certification pre-flight, platform-adapter infrastructure, and the explicit surfacing of
human-gated terminal steps (console cert sign-off, store-page publish/pricing).

**Three-layer structure:**

1. **Cert Pre-Flight Harness** — runs the machine-checkable 55–80% of platform certification
   requirements (directional estimates: Sony ~60–65%, Xbox ~75–80% with GDK Submission
   Validator, Nintendo ~55–60%) and emits a structured `cert-preflight-report`.

2. **Distribution-Adapter Protocol** — mirrors the engine-adapter pattern: capability
   negotiation + fidelity grading (`full | partial | human-gated | none`) + conformance suite.
   `human-gated` fidelity is the fourth value, encoding that the factory's automatable prefix
   is complete and a checklisted human act is surfaced (DI-006).

3. **Verified Distribution CLIs** — `steamcmd` (SteamPipe depot upload, verified against
   Valve partner docs), `butler push` (itch.io, MIT, verified against itch.io/docs/butler/),
   and `fastlane` (iOS/Android, verified against docs.fastlane.tools; only verified actions
   in the allow-list). Unverified/confabulated tool flags are explicitly blocked.

**Human-gated boundary (DI-006):**
- Console cert sign-off (Xbox/PSN/Nintendo): automatable prefix complete; single checklisted
  task surfaced. Factory NEVER automates console cert.
- Store-page publish/pricing (all platforms): automatable upload complete; single checklisted
  task surfaced. Factory NEVER publishes a store page or sets pricing.

**Research grounding:** CAP-009 scope was explicitly moved IN by AAA-RECONCILIATION v2.0 §5.9
and §10 Tier 1; research confabulation-aware: upstream tool confabulations (`steamcmd
Automation Framework 2025`, fake fastlane actions, Sony `GN Tool`, Nintendo `NSC Java GUI`)
are explicitly discarded; all tool behaviors anchored to primary vendor docs.

### 9.2 Design Principles

**Automatable prefix principle.** The factory does 100% of what is scriptable — build
packaging, depot upload, GDK Submission Validator, fastlane signing + upload, cert checklist
evaluation — and surfaces one checklisted human task for each non-automatable terminal step.
This is the same pattern as `replay: none → human playtest` degradation.

**Distribution-adapter as the fifth seam.** The distribution adapter mirrors the engine
adapter: capability negotiation, fidelity grading, conformance suite. Platform lock-in at the
distribution layer is the same structural risk as engine lock-in. The N-API-for-one-concept
problem (steamcmd vs butler vs fastlane vs GDK) is structurally identical to N-engines-for-one-
game-capability.

**Confabulation-safe automation.** The CLIs used (`steamcmd`, `butler`, `fastlane`) are
verified against primary documentation. Unverified commands are blocked at the adapter level
(BC-9.03.003 fastlane allow-list; BC-9.03.002 butler verified subcommands only).

## Section 10: CAP-010 — Compliance Pipeline and AI Disclosure (P1)

### 10.1 Overview

CAP-010 is the factory's compliance automation layer. It covers the machine-checkable half
of the compliance/legal/ratings obligations that apply to an all-genre, all-region,
AI-generated AAA game. The compliance posture follows the same spine/shell split used
throughout the factory:

- **Factory owns (GREEN):** objective IARC questionnaire answers, PEGI-2026 minimum-age
  prediction, probability-disclosure presence and accuracy, spend-cap/playtime/real-name
  config, kompu-gacha pattern detection, privacy default settings, age-gate presence,
  AI disclosure manifest, legal-doc structural completeness.
- **Factory flags for human decision (YELLOW):** candidate sensitive-content regions
  (Germany §86a, China content, Australia simulated-gambling + real-money link).
- **Always human-legal (RED):** loot-box gambling characterization, child-directed
  determination, lawful-basis adequacy, DPIA reasoning, legal-doc substantive sufficiency.

**Regulatory anchors (compliance deadline note D-008):**

| Regulation | Deadline | Factory impact |
|-----------|---------|---------------|
| EU AI Act Art. 50(2) machine-readable marking | **2026-08-02** | `ai-disclosure-manifest` C2PA marks required on all shipped AI-generated audio/image/video/text |
| FTC COPPA 2025 amendment | Compliance **2026-04-22** | `privacy-config-contract` separate consent for ad SDKs; biometric/gov-ID as PII |
| PEGI 2026 interactive-risk categories | **June 2026** (new submissions) | `ratings-submission-manifest` PEGI-2026 minimum-age prediction |
| USK Germany (legally binding) | Ongoing | `compliance-checklist` USK flag; human-legal gate for physical |
| GRAC South Korea (legally binding) | Ongoing | Ratings-submission task: statutory requirement flagged |

**Cross-capability dependency (critical):**

CAP-010 depends on CAP-004 (Pure-Maximal Asset Generation with Auto-Provenance). The
`ai-disclosure-manifest` (BC-10.05.001) is a pure projection of the `asset-provenance-sidecar`
records generated by CAP-004. Specifically:
- CAP-004's `disclosure_class` field (DI-003) is consumed by BC-10.05.001 for EU/Steam/China
  disclosure classification.
- If CAP-004 fails to populate `disclosure_class` on any shipped asset, BC-10.05.001
  emits a DI-003 violation and blocks the compliance pipeline.

### 10.2 Design Principles

**Compliance as versioned data.** The compliance rule set (PEGI-2026 thresholds, COPPA
consent ages, etc.) is versioned data — not hard-coded logic. Rules must be re-validated
per submission cycle (R-016: fast-moving law). Compliance rules are updated by the
`compliance-officer` role, not embedded in factory code.

**Machine-check presence, not adequacy.** The factory is precise about what it can and
cannot verify. It checks PRESENCE of required elements (privacy policy present, age-gate
present, AI disclosure manifest present) and STRUCTURAL COMPLETENESS (all required sections
in legal docs), never ADEQUACY of legal reasoning. This boundary is machine-enforced:
adequacy items are always in `human_legal_gates[]`, never in GREEN items.

**Provenance sidecar as the compliance source of truth.** The `ai-disclosure-manifest` is
not a new source of truth — it is a projection of `asset-provenance-sidecar`. This means
compliance for EU AI Act Art. 50 is directly derived from the same provenance discipline
that manages IP risk (DI-003). One sidecar, two enforcement contexts.

**No confabulated legal claims.** The factory does not assert fake legal instruments.
Research note: ratings-legal-compliance.md corrected ≥6 upstream confabulations (fake EAA
Annex-I exclusion, fake C2PA statutory mandate, fake case names, fake GB/T standard
number). The China AI-labeling manifest is explicitly `[UNVERIFIED]` on standard numbers.

## Section 11: Cross-Capability Dependencies

### 11.1 CAP-010 → CAP-004 (AI Disclosure → Asset Provenance)

BC-10.05.001 (`ai-disclosure-manifest`) is a **pure projection** of `asset-provenance-sidecar`
records produced by the CAP-004 pipeline. The dependency is:

```
CAP-004 (BC-4.xx) --[provenance sidecar]--→ BC-10.05.001 (ai-disclosure-manifest)
  └── disclosure_class field (DI-003)         └── Steam disclosure
                                               └── EU AI Act Art. 50 C2PA marks
                                               └── China AI label manifest
```

Specifically, CAP-004's `disclosure_class` field is the primary input to the manifest's
per-asset classification. If any shipped asset is missing `disclosure_class` in its sidecar,
BC-10.05.001 emits `E-COMP-010` (DI-003 violation) and the compliance pipeline is `BLOCKED`.

This is the **most critical inter-capability dependency** in the CAP-009/010 batch. It
means: implementing CAP-010 AI disclosure compliance at the manifest level requires CAP-004
provenance sidecar to be complete and correct. The factory's "100% provenance coverage"
success metric is not just an IP risk concern — it is the legal precondition for EU AI Act
compliance.

### 11.2 CAP-009 → CAP-010 (Distribution-Readiness → Compliance)

The `distribution-release-pipeline` (BC-9.04.001) references the `compliance-checklist`
status as one of its preconditions. A game with `compliance-checklist.overall_status: BLOCKED`
should not advance to distribution readiness. This is a convergence dimension gate:
`provenance_legal_compliance` must be at least `AMBER` before `distribution_readiness`
can be `GREEN`.

### 11.3 Both CAP-009 and CAP-010 → CAP-007 (Convergence Tracking)

Both capabilities feed the 11-dimension convergence model:

- CAP-009 feeds convergence dimension: `cert_preflight` (tracked via `cert-preflight-report`)
  and `distribution_readiness` (tracked via `distribution-release-pipeline`).
- CAP-010 feeds convergence dimension: `provenance_legal_compliance` (tracked via
  `compliance-checklist`).
