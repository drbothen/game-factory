# BC-11.03.005: Forbidden Dark Pattern — Miscategorized Best-Value Bundle (DP-006)

## Description

Defines the machine-checkable assertion that any IAP bundle or SKU labeled
with a `best_value_tag: true` (or equivalent marketing label such as
"Most Popular", "Best Value", "Recommended") must genuinely have the highest
per-unit expected value (EV) in its category. This is DP-006: the "best
value" decoy SKU, a documented deceptive pricing pattern where a middle-tier
bundle is labeled "best value" but a simple EV calculation would show it is
actually dominated by another option. The FTC Section 5 risk is real; the
test is straightforward arithmetic that the factory can execute directly
against the `iap-catalog` artifact.

## Preconditions

1. An `iap-catalog` artifact exists for this game_id.
2. At least one SKU in the catalog has a `best_value_tag: true` or a
   `marketing_label` field containing "best value", "most popular",
   "recommended", or equivalent.
3. The `iap-catalog` has `unit_price_usd` and `unit_quantity` (or equivalent
   EV-computable fields) for every SKU in the comparison group.

## Postconditions

1. **PASS:** For every SKU with `best_value_tag: true`, its per-unit EV
   (`unit_quantity / unit_price_usd`) is strictly >= the per-unit EV of every
   other SKU in the same category. Ties are permitted (if two SKUs tie for
   best EV, both may be labeled).
2. **VIOLATION:** A SKU has `best_value_tag: true` but another SKU in the
   same category has a higher per-unit EV. Error:
   `BEST_VALUE_LABEL_DECEPTIVE`: the mislabeled SKU, the correctly dominant
   SKU, their per-unit EVs, and the gap are all included in the error.
3. **UNVERIFIABLE:** A SKU has a `marketing_label` that implies best value
   semantics (e.g., "Recommended") but no `best_value_tag` field is present.
   Warning: `IMPLICIT_BEST_VALUE_LABEL_REQUIRES_AUDIT`. The adversarial review
   must manually verify this SKU.
4. **DECOY DETECTED:** A SKU has a per-unit EV that is strictly less than
   both smaller and larger bundles (intentional anchor/decoy pricing).
   Warning: `DECOY_SKU_DETECTED` — not a violation if the decoy is not
   labeled as "best value," but flagged for adversarial review scrutiny.

## Invariants

1. The per-unit EV comparison must be made within the same currency category.
   Consumable gems vs durable-unlock cosmetics are different categories; they
   cannot be compared EV-wise.
2. The factory computes EV as `quantity / price` for consumables and as
   `declared_value / price` for non-consumables where the game declares a
   `declared_value` field. If no EV-comparable field exists, the check
   degrades to a flag for adversarial review.
3. Bundle bonuses (e.g., "buy 1000 gems, get 200 free") must be included in
   the EV calculation. A bundle that nominally provides 1000 gems but includes
   a 200-gem bonus has an effective quantity of 1200 for EV purposes.
4. Limited-time discounts affect EV while active. The EV check uses the
   current active price, not the sticker price. A "best value" label that is
   only valid during a sale period must still hold during the sale.

## Edge Cases

| ID | Description | Expected Behavior |
|----|-------------|-------------------|
| EC-001 | Three consumable gem bundles: 100/$1 (0.010 $/gem), 500/$4 (0.008 $/gem), 2000/$15 (0.0075 $/gem). The 2000-gem bundle has highest EV but middle bundle is labeled "best value" | VIOLATION: `BEST_VALUE_LABEL_DECEPTIVE` on the 500-gem bundle. |
| EC-002 | Same three bundles; largest bundle labeled "best value"; smallest and middle have no label | PASS: the labeled bundle has the highest per-unit EV. |
| EC-003 | All three bundles labeled "best value" | VIOLATION: only the bundle(s) with max EV may be labeled. Bundles with lower EV must not carry the label. |
| EC-004 | Non-consumable skin ($10) labeled "best value" in a category with only one skin | PASS: trivially true (only option in category). |
| EC-005 | Bundle has a limited-time 20% bonus; labeled "best value"; without the bonus it would not be the best value | PASS during the sale. However, if the label persists after the bonus expires, the check will fire at that time. |
| EC-006 | Bundle is labeled "Most Popular" but is not the highest EV | Warning: `IMPLICIT_BEST_VALUE_LABEL_REQUIRES_AUDIT`. "Most popular" implies recommendation but does not technically claim best value — adversarial review must determine if it is deceptive in context. |
