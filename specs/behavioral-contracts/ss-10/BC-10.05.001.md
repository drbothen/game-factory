# BC-10.05.001: AI-Disclosure-Manifest Is a Complete Projection of Provenance Sidecars with C2PA Marks

## Description

The factory emits an `ai-disclosure-manifest` for every shipped game build by projecting
the `asset-provenance-sidecar` records of all shipped AI-generated assets. The manifest
serves three simultaneous obligations: (1) Steam AI Content on Steam disclosure (pre-generated
vs live-generated classification, verified Valve Steamworks announcement Jan 2024);
(2) EU AI Act Article 50(2) machine-readable marking of AI-generated audio/image/video/text
in a detectable format (applies **2 August 2026**, primary-verified: artificialintelligenceact.eu
+ EU AI Act Service Desk timeline); (3) China AI-labeling manifest (direction is real;
specific GB/T standard number is `[UNVERIFIED]` — factory emits a placeholder manifest
flagged for human China-legal review). C2PA Content Credentials are used as the machine-
readable marking format for EU AI Act compliance; C2PA is NOT a statutory mandate but is
the Commission-referenced practice. This BC directly enforces DI-003 (every asset has a
complete provenance sidecar) at the manifest aggregation layer.

## Preconditions

1. All shipped AI-generated assets have a complete `asset-provenance-sidecar` (DI-003,
   BC enforced by CAP-004 pipeline / BC-4.03.002) with fields:
   - `asset_id`
   - `asset_class` (canonical sidecar field; values: `mesh3d`, `texture`, `audio.sfx`,
     `audio.music`, `audio.voice`, `image.concept`, `text.narrative` — per entities.md
     and adapter-protocols.md §3.4)
   - `generated_by_tool` (tool name + version)
   - `generation_date` (ISO-8601)
   - `disclosure_class` (exactly `pre-generated | live-generated | procedural-exempt` —
     canonical closed enum per BC-4.03.002; no other values permitted)
   - `model_id` (generative model identifier)
   - `prompt_log_ref` (reference to prompt/seed used)
   - `license` (tool license / training data license)

   **EU Art. 50 scope projection:** The manifest internally derives an `eu_scope_category`
   field from `asset_class` for EU Art. 50 scope filtering. This is a manifest-local
   projection, NOT a sidecar field. Mapping:
   - `image.concept` → `image`
   - `audio.sfx`, `audio.music`, `audio.voice` → `audio`
   - `text.narrative` → `text`
   - `mesh3d`, `texture` → `3d-mesh` (out of EU Art. 50 image/audio/video/text scope)

   **Steam copilot-tool exemption note:** Code generated by internal development tools
   (e.g., code copilots) is NOT a shipped game asset and never enters the asset pipeline
   or provenance sidecar system. It does not receive a sidecar, has no `disclosure_class`,
   and is exempt from Steam and EU AI Act Art. 50 disclosure per Steam's "internal
   efficiency tools" policy (ratings-legal-compliance.md §6, Valve Steamworks — verified).
   This exemption is a manifest-generation routing decision, not a sidecar enum value.

2. The `game-manifest` (list of all shipped assets) exists for the build version.
3. The target storefronts include at least one of: Steam, EU-market, China.
4. EU AI Act Art. 50 deadline (2026-08-02) is within or before the projected ship window.

## Behavior

1. Factory reads the `game-manifest` and collects all shipped asset IDs.
2. For each asset ID, the factory reads the `asset-provenance-sidecar`.
3. Factory validates that every shipped asset has a complete sidecar (all required fields
   populated). Any asset with a missing or incomplete sidecar generates error `E-COMP-010`:
   "Asset <asset_id> is missing required provenance sidecar field(s): <fields>. DI-003
   violation."
4. Factory emits `ai-disclosure-manifest` (JSON):
   ```json
   {
     "game_id": "<id>",
     "build_version": "<version>",
     "manifest_timestamp": "<ISO-8601>",
     "eu_ai_act_art50_deadline": "2026-08-02",
     "steam_disclosure": {
       "has_pre_generated_ai_content": true|false,
       "has_live_generated_ai_content": true|false,
       "has_procedural_exempt_content": true|false,
       "disclosure_summary": "<text for Steam store-page disclosure>"
     },
     "eu_c2pa_marks": {
       "marking_format": "C2PA-ContentCredentials-1.x",
       "marked_assets": [
         {"asset_id": "<id>",
          "asset_class": "<canonical sidecar value: mesh3d|texture|audio.sfx|audio.music|audio.voice|image.concept|text.narrative>",
          "eu_scope_category": "image|audio|text",
          "c2pa_manifest_ref": "<path_to_c2pa_manifest_file>",
          "generated_by_tool": "<tool>", "model_id": "<model>",
          "disclosure_class": "pre-generated|live-generated|procedural-exempt"}
       ],
       "coverage_count": N,
       "coverage_status": "COMPLETE|PARTIAL|NONE"
     },
     "china_ai_label_manifest": {
       "status": "DRAFT_PENDING_REVIEW",
       "note": "[UNVERIFIED: specific GB/T standard number; factory emits a placeholder manifest; route to China-qualified legal counsel for final labeling requirements]",
       "labeled_assets": ["..."]
     },
     "assets_without_sidecar": [],
     "overall_disclosure_status": "COMPLETE|PARTIAL|BLOCKED"
   }
   ```
5. For `eu_c2pa_marks`:
   - Factory generates a C2PA Content Credentials manifest file for each AI-generated asset
     whose `eu_scope_category` is one of `image|audio|text` (i.e., `asset_class` maps to an
     EU Art. 50 in-scope category per the projection in the Preconditions). The sidecar fields
     `generated_by_tool`, `model_id`, `generation_date`, `prompt_log_ref` are used as the
     C2PA assertion payload.
   - `coverage_status: COMPLETE` iff all shipped EU-scope-category assets with
     `disclosure_class: pre-generated|live-generated` have a C2PA manifest file.
   - Assets with `disclosure_class: procedural-exempt` are NOT AI-generated and are
     excluded from `eu_c2pa_marks.marked_assets`. Their `eu_scope_category` is still
     projected for completeness checking but they are exempt from C2PA marking obligations.
6. `overall_disclosure_status`:
   - `BLOCKED` iff `assets_without_sidecar` is non-empty (DI-003 violation)
   - `PARTIAL` iff `eu_c2pa_marks.coverage_status != COMPLETE` or China manifest is pending review
   - `COMPLETE` iff all assets have sidecars and EU C2PA coverage is COMPLETE (China: pending review
     is acceptable as it is a YELLOW item)

## Postconditions

- `ai-disclosure-manifest.json` exists at `.factory/artifacts/compliance/<game_id>/ai-disclosure-manifest-<build_version>.json`.
- `eu_c2pa_marks.coverage_count` equals the count of shipped AI-generated image/audio/video/text assets.
- `assets_without_sidecar` is empty (any non-empty value → DI-003 violation → BLOCKED status).
- Every shipped AI-generated asset of applicable type has a C2PA Content Credentials file.
- `steam_disclosure.disclosure_summary` is non-empty and usable as input to the Steam AI
  Content disclosure form.

## Invariants

- INV-1 (DI-003): Every shipped asset MUST have a complete provenance sidecar. The manifest
  NEVER suppresses or skips assets with missing sidecars. `assets_without_sidecar` must be
  empty for `overall_disclosure_status: COMPLETE`.
- INV-2: `eu_ai_act_art50_deadline: "2026-08-02"` is a fixed, non-configurable field in the
  manifest. It serves as a compliance deadline reminder. Games shipping after this date with
  `eu_c2pa_marks.coverage_status != COMPLETE` must be flagged as non-compliant.
- INV-3: `disclosure_class` values consumed by this BC are strictly `{pre-generated, live-generated, procedural-exempt}` — the canonical closed enum defined in BC-4.03.002. No other value is valid; any other value arriving on an inbound sidecar is a schema error (E-COMP-010 / DI-003 violation) and sets `overall_disclosure_status: BLOCKED`. Handling by value:
  - `pre-generated`: AI content baked into the build; included in `eu_c2pa_marks.marked_assets` (if EU-scope category); flagged in `steam_disclosure.has_pre_generated_ai_content: true`.
  - `live-generated`: AI content generated at runtime; included in `eu_c2pa_marks.marked_assets` (if EU-scope category); flagged in `steam_disclosure.has_live_generated_ai_content: true`; triggers Steam AO-compliance review note.
  - `procedural-exempt`: Classical procedural generation; excluded from `eu_c2pa_marks.marked_assets`; flagged in `steam_disclosure.has_procedural_exempt_content: true`; Steam exempt per classical-algorithm carve-out.
  Code generated by internal development tools (e.g., code copilots) is NOT a shipped asset, does NOT enter the asset pipeline, and therefore has no sidecar and no `disclosure_class`. The Steam "internal efficiency tools" exemption applies to such tooling at the manifest-routing layer — not as a sidecar enum value.
- INV-4: C2PA is used as the machine-readable marking format for EU AI Act compliance.
  The factory does NOT assert "C2PA is legally mandated"; it uses C2PA as the
  Commission-referenced practice. Compliance determination is human-legal (YELLOW).
- INV-5: China AI labeling manifest is always DRAFT with `[UNVERIFIED]` notice. Factory
  never asserts that the China manifest meets a specific GB/T standard.

## Edge Cases

| EC-ID | Scenario | Expected Result |
|-------|----------|----------------|
| EC-001 | Asset with `disclosure_class: procedural-exempt` in sidecar (e.g., WFC dungeon layout, Houdini PCG terrain) | Excluded from `eu_c2pa_marks.marked_assets`; `steam_disclosure.has_procedural_exempt_content: true`; no C2PA mark generated; DI-003 sidecar still required for audit trail |
| EC-002 | Game has NO AI-generated assets (`ai_generated_content: false`) | All disclosure fields are empty/false; `overall_disclosure_status: COMPLETE`; `steam_disclosure.has_pre_generated_ai_content: false` |
| EC-003 | Asset is a 3D mesh (not in EU Art 50 scope for image/audio/video/text) | 3D mesh excluded from `eu_c2pa_marks`; still requires sidecar for DI-003; noted in `steam_disclosure` section |
| EC-004 | Sidecar has `generated_by_tool: "Stable Audio"` but `model_id: null` | C2PA manifest generated with `model_id: "unknown"`; warning in manifest: "model_id missing from sidecar for asset <id>; C2PA manifest may be incomplete" |
| EC-005 | Game ships after 2026-08-02 with `eu_c2pa_marks.coverage_status: PARTIAL` | Manifest records `eu_ai_act_compliance_risk: true`; compliance-checklist flags as YELLOW for human-legal review |
| EC-006 | `live-generated` AI content at runtime (runtime generative dialogue) | `steam_disclosure.has_live_generated_ai_content: true`; Note in manifest: "Live-generated content requires Steam AO-compliance check for sexual content; ensure runtime guardrails are declared"; flagged YELLOW |

## Verification Properties

- VP-COMP-013: `assets_without_sidecar` is empty in every `overall_disclosure_status: COMPLETE` manifest.
- VP-COMP-014: `eu_c2pa_marks.marked_assets` count equals the count of shipped AI-generated
  assets whose `eu_scope_category` is `image|audio|text` AND whose `disclosure_class` is
  `pre-generated` or `live-generated` (i.e., `procedural-exempt` assets are excluded).
- VP-COMP-015: `eu_ai_act_art50_deadline: "2026-08-02"` is present in every manifest, immutable.
- VP-COMP-016: No `procedural-exempt` asset appears in `eu_c2pa_marks.marked_assets`.
- VP-COMP-017: Every inbound sidecar `disclosure_class` value is a member of
  `{pre-generated, live-generated, procedural-exempt}`. Any out-of-vocabulary value
  triggers E-COMP-010 and sets `overall_disclosure_status: BLOCKED`.

## Cross-Capability Dependencies

This BC depends on CAP-004 (Pure-Maximal Asset Generation with Auto-Provenance):
- CAP-004 mandates that every generated asset has a complete `asset-provenance-sidecar`
  at generation time with `disclosure_class` populated (DI-003).
- This BC consumes the `disclosure_class` field from CAP-004 provenance sidecars
  to classify assets for EU/Steam/China disclosure.
- If CAP-004 fails to populate `disclosure_class` on any asset, this BC generates
  error `E-COMP-010` (DI-003 violation) and sets `overall_disclosure_status: BLOCKED`.
