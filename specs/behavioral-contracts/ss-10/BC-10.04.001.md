# BC-10.04.001: Legal-Doc-Set Is Generated as DRAFT with Structural Completeness Gate and Human-Legal-Review Flag

## Description

The factory generates templated DRAFT legal documents (EULA, Terms of Service, Privacy
Policy, and applicable region addenda) for each game. Every generated document is explicitly
marked "DRAFT — requires legal review before use." The factory gates on: document presence,
required section completeness, and consistency between the Privacy Policy and the
data-collection inventory. Substantive adequacy — whether the terms are legally sufficient,
enforceable, or jurisdiction-complete — is always a human-legal gate. The factory NEVER
asserts that generated documents are legally sufficient. Source: ratings-legal-compliance.md §7.

## Preconditions

1. A `game-profile` exists with `target_regions[]`, `monetization_model`, and
   `ai_generated_content: bool`.
2. A `data-collection-inventory` exists (from `privacy-config-contract`, BC-10.03.001).
3. Legal document templates exist in the factory template store for: EULA, ToS, Privacy Policy,
   and each applicable region addendum (GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, COPPA, China).
4. Each template declares its required section headings.

## Behavior

1. Factory selects applicable templates based on `target_regions[]` and `game-profile`.
2. For each template, factory populates:
   - Game name, company placeholder, effective date placeholder
   - AI-content disclosure clause (present iff `ai_generated_content: true`)
   - UGC clause (present iff `ugc_enabled: true` in game-profile)
   - Data collection sections: populated from `data-collection-inventory`
   - Region addenda: included iff region in `target_regions[]`
   - Refund policy placeholder (factual: Steam 2-hour/14-day; EU 14-day withdrawal; Australian ACL)
   - EOL/preservation statement placeholder
3. Stamp every generated document with header:
   ```
   ⚠️  DRAFT — REQUIRES LEGAL REVIEW BEFORE USE
   This document was auto-generated by game-factory. It is NOT legally reviewed and
   NOT ready for publication. Route to qualified legal counsel before shipping.
   ```
4. Machine-gate structural completeness:
   - Check: every required section heading is present in the generated document.
   - Check: Privacy Policy data collection sections include all recipients from `data-collection-inventory`.
   - Check: EULA includes AI-content disclosure clause iff `ai_generated_content: true`.
   - Check: Region addenda are included for all `target_regions[]` that require them.
5. Emit `legal-doc-set` manifest (JSON):
   ```json
   {
     "game_id": "<id>",
     "build_version": "<version>",
     "documents": [
       {"doc_type": "EULA|ToS|PrivacyPolicy|GDPRAddendum|CCPAAddendum|COPPAAddendum|ChinaAddendum",
        "draft_path": "<path>",
        "template_version": "<ver>",
        "structural_status": "PASS|FAIL",
        "missing_sections": [],
        "inventory_consistency_status": "PASS|FAIL|N/A",
        "draft_marker_present": true}
     ],
     "human_legal_review_required": true,
     "human_legal_review_status": "outstanding",
     "overall_status": "STRUCTURALLY_COMPLETE|STRUCTURALLY_INCOMPLETE"
   }
   ```
6. A `human-gated-legal-review-task` is always generated (BC-10.04.001 postcondition).

## Postconditions

- Draft documents exist at `.factory/artifacts/compliance/<game_id>/legal-docs/<build_version>/`.
- Every document has the DRAFT header marker.
- `legal-doc-set` manifest exists and validates against `legal-doc-set-v1.schema.json`.
- `human_legal_review_required: true` in every emitted manifest — this field is immutable.
- A `human-gated-legal-review-task` is in `compliance-checklist.human_tasks[]`.

## Invariants

- INV-1: `human_legal_review_required` is ALWAYS `true`. No configuration or override can
  suppress it. Emitting a legal-doc-set without this field is a schema validation error.
- INV-2: Every generated document has the DRAFT header marker. A document without the marker
  is treated as a schema validation FAIL.
- INV-3: AI-content disclosure clause is present in EULA iff `ai_generated_content: true`.
  For game-factory (pure-maximal asset generation), `ai_generated_content` is always `true`
  unless explicitly declared `false` for a specific game.
- INV-4: The factory NEVER populates legal-opinion-dependent fields (adequacy of lawful basis,
  dispute resolution jurisdiction, limitation-of-liability amounts). These are left as
  `[REQUIRES LEGAL REVIEW: <description>]` placeholders.

## Edge Cases

| EC-ID | Scenario | Expected Result |
|-------|----------|----------------|
| EC-001 | `target_regions` includes China | China-specific EULA addendum generated with placeholders for local-publisher terms, banhao ref, AI-labeling note; structural check includes China-required sections |
| EC-002 | EULA template missing required "License Grant" section | Structural FAIL: "EULA missing required section: 'License Grant'"; `overall_status: STRUCTURALLY_INCOMPLETE` |
| EC-003 | Privacy Policy generated but data-collection-inventory lists an ad SDK not referenced in the Policy | Inventory-consistency FAIL: "Ad SDK '<sdk>' appears in data-collection-inventory but not in Privacy Policy data-recipients section" |
| EC-004 | `ai_generated_content: false` (unusual for game-factory) | AI-content disclosure clause NOT included in EULA; note in manifest: "ai_generated_content: false — AI-disclosure clause omitted" |
| EC-005 | Human-legal review completed; legal-doc-set updated | `human_legal_review_status` can be updated to `complete` by a human (legal-counsel role); DRAFT marker is replaced by human in the actual reviewed document |
